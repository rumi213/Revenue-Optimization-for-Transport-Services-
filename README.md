# Revenue Optimizatation For Transportation Services

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes NYC Taxi trip data to determine whether a passenger's payment method (Cash vs. Credit Card) has a significant impact on taxi driver revenue. By applying exploratory data analysis (EDA) and statistical hypothesis testing, the study aims to provide data-driven insights that can help taxi drivers maximize their earnings.

The analysis investigates tipping behavior, fare amounts, and overall revenue patterns associated with different payment methods.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The primary goal of this project is to answer the following question:

> **Do passengers who pay with credit cards generate higher revenue for taxi drivers compared to passengers who pay with cash?**

To answer this, we perform:

* Data cleaning and preprocessing
* Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* Descriptive statistics
* Hypothesis testing
* Revenue comparison across payment types

---

## 🔍 Methodology

### 1. Data Preprocessing

* Load and inspect dataset
* Handle missing values
* Filter relevant features

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis

* Analyze payment method distribution
* Compare fare amounts across payment types
* Visualize tip and revenue patterns
* Identify trends and anomalies

### 3. Hypothesis Testing

#### Null Hypothesis (H₀)

There is **no significant difference** in revenue generated between cash and credit card payments.

#### Alternative Hypothesis (H₁)

There **is a significant difference** in revenue generated between cash and credit card payments.

Statistical tests are performed to determine whether observed differences are statistically significant.
